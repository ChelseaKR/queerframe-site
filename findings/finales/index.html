35% of queer TV regulars who die are killed in a season finale

Data as of November 6, 2026. Analysis by Queer Frame of LezWatch.TV's character data and TVmaze's episode dates.

When a queer character who is a regular on a TV show dies, it happens in a season finale 35% of the time (50 of 142 deaths we could place in an episode). Only 8% of those shows' episodes are season finales, so that is about 4.4 times what chance would give. Guest characters die in a finale 7% of the time, close to the 8% chance would give.

This page names no show, no character and no episode, so it spoils nothing. It counts deaths; it does not list them.

Deaths are spread evenly across a show's seasons

Deaths are not bunched at the start or the end of a show's run. The first season holds 27% of placed deaths and 28% of the episodes. The pattern is within a season, not across the run.

Series finales, a smaller sample

In shows that have ended, 10% of placed deaths (32 of 329) fall in the series finale, against 3% expected by chance: about 3 times as often. That rests on few deaths, so read it as a hint, not a finding.

How we counted

A death
A date in LezWatch.TV's death-date field for a character, counted in the show whose years for that character include the year of the death. A character who later comes back still counts. The snapshot holds 673 such deaths.
Placed in an episode
We matched each death date to TVmaze's episode air dates. A death is placed only when exactly one episode of that show aired on that date, or, failing that, exactly one within a day. When two or more aired the same day (a double bill, or a season released all at once), or none did, the death is left out. That leaves 431 deaths on 266 shows. Specials are left out.
A regular
LezWatch.TV's role for the character in that show: regular, recurring or guest.
A season finale
The highest-numbered regular episode TVmaze lists for that season. A series finale is the last episode of the last season of a show TVmaze marks as ended.
Expected by chance
For each death, the chance that a random episode of its own show is a season finale (its number of seasons over its number of episodes), averaged over the deaths in the group. Long seasons make a finale rarer; short ones make it more common.
Dates
LezWatch.TV data from the Queer Frame snapshot of November 6, 2026; TVmaze episode lists read on November 6, 2026. These figures are fixed as of those dates and are not updated with each nightly build.

Caveats

  • LezWatch.TV is a volunteer-run database. It covers more US shows than any other country, and it is more complete for the shows its community watched. Older shows were filled in unevenly.
  • LezWatch.TV dates a death to the day the episode aired. It may record the episode where a death is revealed rather than the one where it happens, and an air date can now and then match the wrong episode by chance.
  • Only deaths we could place are counted. Most deaths in seasons released all at once can't be placed, so streaming shows are under-represented.
  • Guest deaths include victims of the week on crime and medical shows, which is why the figures are split by role.
  • A season still airing may not have its finale listed yet, so its last listed episode is counted as its finale.
